# C1 Governance, Policy & Authority

## Control Objective

This chapter addresses establishing incident response as a governed organizational capability before an incident occurs, with management commitment, a maintained plan, pre-delegated authority to act, and a clear understanding of the legal and contractual obligations an incident may trigger.

---

## C1.1 Policy & Plan

An approved policy and a maintained, reachable plan must exist so that responders are not inventing process during a crisis.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **1.1.1** | **Verify that** an incident response policy approved by senior management defines the purpose, scope, and management commitment for incident response. | 1 |
| **1.1.2** | **Verify that** a documented incident response plan describes how incidents are reported, assessed, contained, recovered from, and closed, and identifies the role responsible for each activity. | 1 |
| **1.1.3** | **Verify that** the incident response plan defines what the organization treats as a security event and as a security incident. | 1 |
| **1.1.4** | **Verify that** the incident response plan is reviewed at least annually, with the review date and approver recorded. | 1 |
| **1.1.5** | **Verify that** copies of the incident response plan and the incident contact list are accessible to responders when the organization's primary network, identity provider, and document systems are unavailable or untrusted. | 1 |
| **1.1.6** | **Verify that** the incident response plan is updated after significant incidents, exercise findings, and material changes to the organization, its technology, or its obligations. | 2 |
| **1.1.7** | **Verify that** the incident response plan is version controlled, with a change history showing what changed, when, and who approved it. | 2 |
| **1.1.8** | **Verify that** the incident response plan documents its interfaces with crisis management, business continuity, and disaster recovery plans, including the criteria for invoking each and who holds overall command when more than one is active. | 2 |

---

## C1.2 Authority & Decision Rights

Responders must hold the authority to act quickly, and high-impact decisions must have owners identified in advance.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **1.2.1** | **Verify that** a named individual or role is accountable for the incident response capability, with at least one designated alternate. | 1 |
| **1.2.2** | **Verify that** the incident lead has documented, pre-approved authority to take urgent containment actions, including disconnecting systems, disabling accounts, and blocking network traffic, without seeking further approval at the time of the incident. | 1 |
| **1.2.3** | **Verify that** decisions with significant business impact, including shutting down production services, engaging law enforcement, making public statements, and responding to extortion demands, each have a designated decision owner and alternate. | 2 |
| **1.2.4** | **Verify that** the authority of responders to access systems, accounts, and data for investigative purposes is documented and has been reviewed by the legal and privacy functions. | 2 |
| **1.2.5** | **Verify that** documented criteria define when an incident is escalated to executive leadership and to the governing body. | 2 |
| **1.2.6** | **Verify that** the governing body receives a report on significant incidents and the state of the incident response capability at least annually. | 3 |

---

## C1.3 Legal, Regulatory & Contractual Obligations

Notification and handling obligations must be known before an incident starts the clock on them.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **1.3.1** | **Verify that** the organization has identified the legal and regulatory incident notification obligations that apply to it. | 1 |
| **1.3.2** | **Verify that** a register of incident notification obligations records, for each legal, regulatory, and contractual obligation, the recipient, the trigger, the deadline, and the required content. | 2 |
| **1.3.3** | **Verify that** the register of notification obligations is reviewed at least annually and when the organization enters a new jurisdiction, sector, or material contract. | 2 |
| **1.3.4** | **Verify that** legal counsel with incident response experience has been identified in advance and is listed in the incident contact list. | 2 |
| **1.3.5** | **Verify that** cyber insurance conditions that affect incident response, including notification deadlines, approved vendor panels, and consent requirements for incurring costs, are documented in the incident response plan. | 2 |
| **1.3.6** | **Verify that** the organization has documented how legal privilege over incident investigations is to be handled, including when counsel directs investigative work and how resulting reports are distributed. | 3 |

---

## C1.4 Scope & Risk Alignment

The capability must cover everything the organization depends on and must be prioritized by the organization's actual risks.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **1.4.1** | **Verify that** the scope of the incident response capability covers every environment the organization operates or depends on, including cloud, SaaS, remote endpoints, operational technology, and subsidiaries, with any exclusion documented and risk-accepted. | 2 |
| **1.4.2** | **Verify that** the incident scenarios used to prioritize playbooks and exercises are derived from the organization's risk assessment and current threat intelligence. | 2 |
| **1.4.3** | **Verify that** the incident response capability is assessed by a party independent of the incident response function at a defined interval. | 3 |
