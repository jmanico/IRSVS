# C10 Communication, Notification & Information Sharing

## Control Objective

This chapter addresses getting accurate, timely, and appropriately protected information to the right people inside and outside the organization, and meeting legal notification obligations.

IRSVS does not state which notification laws apply to an organization or what they require. Chapter C1.3 requires the organization to know its own obligations, and this chapter verifies that the organization can meet them. Appendix D gives an informative summary of commonly encountered deadlines.

---

## C10.1 Internal Communication

Internal stakeholders must be kept informed on a predictable rhythm, without incident details spreading further than they need to.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **10.1.1** | **Verify that** the incident response plan identifies which internal stakeholders are informed of incidents and by whom. | 1 |
| **10.1.2** | **Verify that** the frequency of status updates to stakeholders is defined for each severity level. | 2 |
| **10.1.3** | **Verify that** incident information is shared internally on a need-to-know basis with handling restrictions marked. | 2 |
| **10.1.4** | **Verify that** guidance for the workforce during a significant incident, covering expected actions and restrictions on external discussion, has been prepared in advance. | 2 |

---

## C10.2 Regulatory & Legal Notification

Notification obligations must be assessed for every incident and met on time, with a record that shows it.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **10.2.1** | **Verify that** an assessment of whether notification obligations are triggered is performed and recorded for each incident. | 1 |
| **10.2.2** | **Verify that**, for each applicable notification obligation, a procedure identifies the responsible role, the content template, and the submission method. | 2 |
| **10.2.3** | **Verify that** notification deadlines are tracked against the time that started each obligation's clock. | 2 |
| **10.2.4** | **Verify that** regulatory and legal notifications are reviewed by legal counsel before submission. | 2 |
| **10.2.5** | **Verify that** copies of submitted notifications and any acknowledgments are retained in the incident record. | 2 |
| **10.2.6** | **Verify that** the notification process supports staged reporting, such as an initial notice followed by updates and a final report, where an obligation requires it. | 2 |
| **10.2.7** | **Verify that** determinations of whether an incident is material or significant for disclosure purposes follow a documented process, with the decision makers, the information considered, and the time of determination recorded. | 3 |

---

## C10.3 Affected Parties & Public Communication

People affected by an incident must be told what they need to know to protect themselves, and public statements must be controlled and accurate.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **10.3.1** | **Verify that** a procedure for notifying affected individuals and customers defines the criteria, approval, and delivery channels. | 2 |
| **10.3.2** | **Verify that** notifications to affected parties describe what happened, what information was involved, what the organization is doing, and what recipients should do. | 2 |
| **10.3.3** | **Verify that** designated spokespersons are identified and that the workforce is instructed to refer media inquiries to them. | 2 |
| **10.3.4** | **Verify that** pre-approved holding statements exist for the organization's prioritized incident scenarios. | 2 |
| **10.3.5** | **Verify that** external communications about an incident are approved by defined roles before release. | 2 |
| **10.3.6** | **Verify that** public statements are checked against confirmed findings in the incident record before release and are corrected when those findings change. | 3 |
| **10.3.7** | **Verify that** capacity to handle inquiries from affected parties at scale has been arranged in advance. | 3 |

---

## C10.4 Law Enforcement & Information Sharing

Engagement with authorities and peers must follow agreed rules about what is shared, with whom, and under what handling restrictions.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **10.4.1** | **Verify that** documented criteria define when law enforcement is engaged and which role is authorized to do so. | 2 |
| **10.4.2** | **Verify that** incident information shared externally is marked with handling restrictions using a recognized scheme such as the Traffic Light Protocol, and that markings on received information are honored. | 2 |
| **10.4.3** | **Verify that** a documented policy defines what incident information may be shared with trusted communities, how it is sanitized, and who approves sharing. | 3 |
