# C15 Post-Incident Activity & Continuous Improvement

## Control Objective

This chapter addresses ensuring that every incident and exercise leaves the organization measurably better prepared, through structured review, tracked corrective action, and metrics that management acts on.

---

## C15.1 Post-Incident Review

Lessons must be identified while memories are fresh, aimed at systemic causes, and converted into completed changes.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **15.1.1** | **Verify that** a post-incident review is conducted for every significant incident and documents what happened, what worked, and what should improve. | 1 |
| **15.1.2** | **Verify that** post-incident reviews are held within a defined time after incident closure. | 2 |
| **15.1.3** | **Verify that** post-incident reviews identify root causes and contributing factors, including control failures and detection gaps. | 2 |
| **15.1.4** | **Verify that** the post-incident review procedure directs reviews toward process and system causes instead of individual blame. | 2 |
| **15.1.5** | **Verify that** corrective actions from post-incident reviews are assigned owners and due dates and are tracked to completion. | 2 |
| **15.1.6** | **Verify that** a final report for each significant incident records the timeline, root cause, impact, response actions, and lessons identified. | 2 |
| **15.1.7** | **Verify that** lessons from incidents result in documented updates to the plan, playbooks, detection content, and training. | 2 |
| **15.1.8** | **Verify that** lower-severity incidents and near misses are reviewed in aggregate at a defined frequency to identify recurring patterns. | 3 |
| **15.1.9** | **Verify that** overdue corrective actions from incidents and exercises are escalated to management. | 3 |

---

## C15.2 Metrics & Reporting

The performance of the capability must be measured and reported so that management can see whether it is improving.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **15.2.1** | **Verify that** incident metrics are collected, including incident counts by category and severity and the times to detect, contain, and recover. | 2 |
| **15.2.2** | **Verify that** incident metrics are reported to management at a defined frequency. | 2 |
| **15.2.3** | **Verify that** improvement targets for incident response performance are set and tracked using incident metrics. | 3 |
| **15.2.4** | **Verify that** the impact of each significant incident, including estimated cost and effort, is recorded. | 3 |
| **15.2.5** | **Verify that** incident trends are provided as input to the organization's risk assessment. | 3 |

---

## C15.3 Records Retention

Incident records must be kept long enough to meet legal obligations and to support later review.

| # | Description | Level |
| :--------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---: |
| **15.3.1** | **Verify that** incident records are retained for a defined period that meets legal and regulatory requirements. | 2 |
